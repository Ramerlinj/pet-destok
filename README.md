# Pet Destok

Pet Destok is a little panda that lives on your desktop. It walks around your screen, takes breaks, and keeps you company with a few motivational phrases.

You don't have to do much — just leave it running and let the panda do its thing!

## How to interact with your panda

- **Left-click and drag** the panda to move it wherever you want.
- **Click** the panda to make it greet you and show a phrase.
- The panda moves around your screen on its own.
- Every now and then, it may stop to sit or take a nap.

That's pretty much it. Enjoy having a little companion on your desktop!

## How to run it

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the project with **Godot 4.7**.
3. Press the Play button in the editor.

Have fun with your new desktop companion! 🐼
